## Evaluate research by what it changes in future creation

| Question | Strong output | Weak output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What did we recover? | Exact identity, source checksum, functioning chain and one reproducible capture | Hundreds of file names and unverifiable inferred effects |
| What is a unique mechanism? | Source birth/state/attribute/deletion with precise provenance | Generic velocity + noise particle equations |
| Why do old results feel wrong? | One source-vs-old-runtime temporal difference isolated | “Shader needs to be more cinematic” without matching camera/input |
| What can we reuse? | Extracted operator/control with correct version, small test and constraints | A beautiful but unrelated procedural effect |
| What does the artist prefer? | Specific author-selected comparison with accepted/rejected reason | Agent self-rating or vague black/white/cyberpunk keywords |
| What should remain private? | Rights-checked ID, public-safe principle; local original preserved | Commercial source, full parameter presets, licensed music pushed publicly |

## Minimum next experiment — one controlled 6–8-second sequence

Choose ONE original source window already successfully evaluated. Keep the original Blender version, music control values, camera, frame count and output aspect. Capture **source vs existing GPU/OF implementation** at identical timing. At least annotate:

1. **Birth/deletion:** growth distribution and ID survival, not just total points.
2. **Motion:** coherent source pathways / Curl domain / speed cadence, frame-to-frame difference.
3. **Spatial composition:** near/far arrangement, local density, characteristic voids and view/camera mismatch.
4. **Presentation:** separate material/instancing/compositor and audio mapping differences from simulation differences.

If it is impossible to get original audio/artist-approved camera, label the output as `NUMERICAL_ONLY` or `GEOMETRY_ONLY`; do not manufacture a visual comparison. Existing 482 lifetime checks should not be repeated as a substitute for next-stage coverage.

## Three-line reviewer output

- **Confirmed:** cite one actual source/test artifact and the specific scope.
- **Still missing:** cite the first real missing output rather than expanding tasks.
- **Next action:** one concrete source-aligned capture or verification; no new substitute style.

## Style graduation test (independent of code correctness)

To promote a style card to `REUSABLE`, supply:
- Original/reference and proposed variant video or frame pair, plus author-selected version;
- 2–3 invariants that the selected variation preserved and two rejected shortcuts;
- Stable inputs / creative controls, rights conditions and one documented failed case;
- Actual user feedback, not assistant-selected approval.

A `MECHANISM_TESTED` GPU kernel **never automatically graduates** an artistic style card.

## Confidentiality

Private manifests, raw .blend/.als files, commercial shader and music nodes, licensed assets and full paths remain in approved local/controlled storage. Public Git only gets redacted source identifiers, summary and permitted screenshots.
