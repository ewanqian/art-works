## Reusable visual language

1. **Contours / isophase lines:** restrained monochrome spatial curves, readable at stage distance.
2. **Signal degradation:** spatial image fracture, compression blocks and deliberate discontinuity, not constant random glitch.
3. **Observation grids:** authored camera/media plates, coordinates and source-aware metadata; never invent sensor readings.
4. **Motion afterimages:** one licensed motion source sampled at staggered times; GPU-instanced rather than duplicated application timelines.
5. **Neural / distributed networks:** artistic simulation, explicitly distinct from measured scalp EEG or neuronal spikes.
6. **Light as structure:** key, rim, uplight, downlight, orbiting light, scanning light and strategic removal.

## Temporal grammar

- **20–200 ms:** transient / local response. Avoid unbounded high-intensity strobing.
- **0.5–8 s:** phrase-level spatial motion, material reveals and geometric changes.
- **16–120 s:** authored act, tonal/visual identity and contrast.

Sound and image share events, but are not compelled to react identically to every beat. Default camera: **HOLD** until explicitly cued; preserve authored picture ratios.

## Aesthetic constraints

Reject unedited preset-parade looks, generic cyberpunk and decorative spectrum bars. Prefer intentionally composed space, grayscale/neutral contrasts, restrained type, clear depth, atmosphere and selective decisive color. Reuse is methodological; each work's scene design is authored again.
