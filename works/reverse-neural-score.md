# 反向神经谱 / Reverse Neural Score

**Record date:** 2026-11-09  
**Status:** **PROPOSED / CREATIVE RESEARCH**. Neither a completed work nor a confirmed scientific reconstruction.  
**Related near-term performance research:** [CRITICAL FIELD / Performable Music](https://github.com/ewanqian/performable-music/tree/main/performances/2026-12-03-phase-01).

## Central proposition

Instead of only measuring brain activity to control sound, a performer makes musical and spatial decisions by **hand gesture, foot controls and an authored timeline**, and an **imagined neural field** responds. That field may feed bounded decisions back into the following musical phrase.

This is a poetic inversion of the brain-computer interface: **music → imagined listening/expectation response → performed audio/visual feedback**.

This is **not** a system for inferring a listener's actual EEG, emotions, memories or individual neural firing from music. Any future true EEG data must be distinguished as measured, recorded or simulated, with device, sampling and processing provenance.

## Artistic form

- **Input:** gesture and manual timeline cue; optionally measured EEG after explicit source verification.
- **Transformation:** excitation, inhibition, propagation, expectation, latency and memory decay expressed as invented musical state variables.
- **Image:** a volumetric neural mesh, travelling impulses, contours, slices, annotated observation space and differentiated light.
- **Music:** original club-oriented percussion, rhythmic subtraction, resonance and structured return.
- **Agency:** performer makes decisive structural changes; no unattended AI improvisation controls the loudness or final scene state.

## Short proposed score (experiment, not final performance)

| Phase | Performer decision | Music consequence | Image consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ACQUIRE | Reveal one source | Sparse tactile pulse | Thin brain-like spatial structure |
| INHIBIT | Hold or subtract | Drum/bass withdrawal | Connections contract; light recedes |
| PROPAGATE | Gesture range change | Pitched percussion dialogue | Delayed local pulses, contour flows |
| OVERRIDE | Explicit foot/button cue | Deliberate rhythmic return | One authored large-scale transformation |
| AFTERIMAGE | Release | Motif returns in altered form | Time-shifted motion traces and darkness |
