# 无需进一步输入 / No Further Input Required

**Work identity:** original moving-image / generative-system artwork.  
**Record updated:** 2026-11-09.  
**Status:** Existing prototype and public exhibition references exist; proposed new audiovisual forms remain **IN DEVELOPMENT**, not automatically a completed film.

## Artistic proposition

A system continues to classify, connect and transform material when fresh external input is reduced or withdrawn. Its own trace becomes later input: an inquiry into feedback, autonomy, memory, differentiation and the limits of the command interface.

## Proposed dramaturgy (not an assertion of finished footage)

1. **Origin** — one small original condition, restrained space and signal.
2. **Differentiation** — forms diverge into discernible behaviours.
3. **Relation** — memory, contacts and event dependencies become spatial.
4. **Evolution** — internal history changes the next state.
5. **Self-Input** — the system processes its own accumulated traces.

A single-channel **8–10 minute** treatment with dark space, 3D lattice and restrained pale geometry is a creative direction. Runtime, soundtrack, edit, final duration, screening version and any claim of self-learning require separate verification. Do not depict a simple weighted transition log as trained intelligence.
