# Artwork Composition Architect / 从组件到成熟作品

Start from [Artworks System 01](../../../plans/2026-11-09-artworks-compounding-system.md), actual source/selected frames, the art-works style cards and user feedback. Do not start with a new technical stack, a generic particle visual, or 20 scene concepts.

**Do not collapse three levels:** A reusable *mechanism* (e.g. Drop Flow lifetime), a *visual treatment* (e.g. structural line reveal), and a *complete artwork* are not the same.

## A. Locate the strongest real material

Choose one source-backed technique and one author-approved musical/visual fragment, or label the proposal as untested if no accepted fragment exists. Prefer high-value existing material to new renders. Ask what the technique *means through action*: accumulation, emergence, decay, observation, transformation, passage, apparent scale, human intervention.

## B. Design a dramatic transformation, not a preset showreel

Outline:
- **World:** a space/mechanism with a perceivable rule.
- **Constraint:** what the system cannot yet do or cannot indefinitely maintain.
- **Change:** what specifically breaks, builds, moves, disappears or reorganizes.
- **Consequence:** why the viewer/performer notices it and why the next passage must happen.
- **Resolution:** an authored ending, transition or new rule. Silence and sustained stillness are legitimate states.

Do not add decorative scientific labels, fake measurement, constant full-field strobes, automatic camera drift, or filler beat reactions.

## C. Compose in three coupled but distinct timescales

1. **Micro (frame/tick):** source simulation, material scan, local motion.
2. **Meso (phrase/shot):** 2–30s spatial transformations, revealing a structure or changing point density with purpose.
3. **Macro (work/act):** 30s to longer musical/dramatic events with anticipation, contrast, return or inversion.

Audio must survive separate listening. Visual should survive short silent viewing. Joint music-to-world cues need event IDs/replay if realtime, but do not force every note onto a visible event.

## D. Make stage scale meaningful

Specify the viewer distance, authored aspect, near/far geometry, front/ceiling relation where present, light hierarchy and what remains readable. More GPU points or wider LED pixel count is not artistic depth. Prefer one powerful 3D transformation with an authored camera to dozens of arbitrary loops.

## E. Use actual components with stated readiness

Read the [component registry](../../../assets/artworks-component-registry.v1.json). Use PACKAGED or TESTED components for the production core. PROPOSED/ARTIST_RECALLED elements are only sketches; use existing approved offline video placeholders if source recovery is incomplete. Keep Simulation Zone/source-tick fidelity separate from live performance options.

## F. Output one decision-ready plan

Deliver **one** selected 30–90 second source-led passage, not ten new works:
- compositional proposition and viewer's experience;
- 3–4 readable moments and why each follows;
- exact source components/status, licensed media and music excerpt;
- Blender still/clip to make now, optional GPU realtime layer later;
- two meaningful A/B alternatives, one deliberately rejected shortcut;
- proof: capture audio-only, silent-video and coupled performance, author selects.

When the user requests a longer show, expand only after one short passage survives both artist review and real runtime/venue testing. Unfinished show concepts must remain PROPOSED, not completed works.
