# Artworks Style Memory / 将作品经验转化为可调用的视觉判断

Read `references/style-cards.md` in **this skill directory** first. Then consult the dated first-person accounts in `lineages/`, project-specific evidence, and local original frames when available.

**Style is not a preset.** Preserve *why* a particular author-selected image worked: time, geometry, spatial framing, sound relationship, restraint, light and authorship. A public oral account is evidence of the artist's remembered intent, not of the exact Blender node recipe.

## For each new creative brief

1. **Choose one lineage** as the base and state why; if multiple are combined, keep separate modules. Never treat KASHIWA Ice, Amb, Drop Flow, NFIR and REACTOR as visually interchangeable.
2. **Extract three layers:**
   - `Invariant`: what gives the work its identity (e.g. surviving source per-frame feedback; deliberate reveal of coherent machine topology).
   - `Flexible`: artist-controllable parameters (camera, density, selective illumination, phrase timing, audio source).
   - `Not this`: forbidden shortcuts (generic random network, arbitrary noise and respawn, fake science UI, every beat flashes).
3. Produce one **art-direction mini-card**: scene/viewing context, compositional grammar, movement/time grammar, light/material, sound/control relationship, 2-3 test shots, 2 rejection examples, source/evidence tier.
4. Before calling anything `approved style`, show **actual captured output**, ideally a 3–8-second loop and the corresponding stills, plus comparison with an original frame under meaningful conditions. The artist's real selection/feedback establishes the accepted signature; an Agent's confidence or numeric score does not.
5. For new designs, avoid exact reconstruction of another collaborator's proprietary work. Reuse the artist's transferable methods; confirm media rights and project credits before release.

## Evidence tiers for style memory

- `ARTIST_RECALLED`: from the artist's first-person account; preserve wording and uncertainty.
- `SOURCE_OBSERVED`: original scene/node/media demonstrates a mechanism.
- `TESTED`: run under stated reproduction inputs.
- `ARTIST_SELECTED`: author compared outputs and explicitly kept/rejected a result.

A skill card may remain useful at `ARTIST_RECALLED`; **never** silently upgrade that to an approved signature or claim the exact source formula is known. Keep exact private parameters in local evidence, not public Git.

## Minimum output

**Base lineage → three invariants → allowed variations → avoid → 2/3 shots to render → author decision needed.** When asked for 9 deliberately different visual or lighting options, activate the separate `nine-way-visual-audition` skill.
